# Agentboxx Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors and participants in the Agentboxx community, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Community Standards

We expect all participants to:

- **Be respectful and collaborative:** Listen with an open mind, give and receive constructive feedback gracefully, and treat others with courtesy.
- **Focus on the community's goals:** Work toward reliable, tamper-evident tooling and shared learning.
- **Respect privacy and boundaries:** Do not disclose private details or sensitive information without consent.

Behaviors that are not acceptable include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or derogatory language in issues, discussions, or pull requests.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or bad-faith interactions.
- Publishing private communications or credentials publicly.

## Enforcement and Reporting

If you encounter or witness unacceptable conduct within the Agentboxx community, please contact the maintainers at **prathmeshkokate775@gmail.com**.

All reports are reviewed promptly, confidentially, and fairly. The maintainers will take appropriate action, which may include a private warning or restricting participation in the repository and discussions.
